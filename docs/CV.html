Summary

Meta-scientist and forensic research analyst who detects fabricated data, statistical errors, and research misconduct in published medical and clinical research. PhD candidate in Quantitative Psychology and Neuroscience at the University of Connecticut with expertise in meta-analysis, clinical trial methodology, and evidence synthesis, and creator of Audit Clinical Evidence (ACE), which audits published clinical trials for research integrity violations.
